# Privacy Policy – Mole Munch
Last updated: 3 October 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The German version (Datenschutzerklärung) is legally binding.

Stephan Kopp

Rhönstr. 20, 63843 Niedernberg, Germany

Email: apple-dev@stephankopp.de

## 2. General

Mole Munch is a game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. We process as little personal data as possible. There is no user account, and we do not use any analytics or crash reporting services.

## 3. Data stored locally

Game progress (completed levels), settings (sound, language) and the status of the “Remove ads” purchase are stored only locally on your device. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app; you can delete your game progress in the settings.

## 4. Advertising (iOS/iPadOS only): Google AdMob

The iOS version shows ads between levels (interstitials) from the Google AdMob service, operated by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). There are no ads on the Mac.

**Data processed:** When ads are requested and shown, the following may be processed, among other things: the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you have allowed tracking, the IP address, device and software information (e.g. model, operating system, language), approximate location (derived from the IP address), app information, and data on interactions with ads (impression, click) and for error diagnosis.

**Purposes:** Serving and billing ads, fraud prevention, measuring and improving ads, error diagnosis.

**Legal basis:** Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG), which you give via the consent form (Google UMP) and the iOS tracking prompt. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are loaded, or no ads are requested at all, where legally permitted.

**Non-personalised ads:** Without consent to personalised ads, Google only shows ads that are not based on your user profile.

**Transfers to third countries:** Google may transfer data to the USA and other countries. For Google, there is an adequacy decision of the European Commission under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; standard contractual clauses apply in addition.

**More information:** Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy; how Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Withdrawal:** You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: in the app under Settings > “Privacy settings” (shown where required by law) and in the iOS settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can also turn off ads permanently with the “Remove ads” purchase.

## 5. In-app purchases

The “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Apple. Payment data is processed exclusively by Apple; we do not receive it. Apple’s privacy policy applies. To restore purchases, the app asks Apple for the purchase status.

## 6. No analytics or crash reporting services

We do not use any services for usage analytics or crash reporting. If you have agreed to this in the iOS settings, Apple may share crash data with us (App Store Connect).

## 7. This website

The website for the app (stephankopp.github.io) is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the pages, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular your IP address, and stores it in server logs in order to deliver the pages and protect against abuse. We ourselves do not use cookies or analytics services on the website.

**Legal basis:** Our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Transfers to third countries:** GitHub may transfer data to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; standard contractual clauses apply in addition. More information: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## 8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to withdraw any consent given at any time (Art. 7(3)). To exercise these rights, contact apple-dev@stephankopp.de. Please note that we ourselves hold hardly any personal data; requests about advertising data are best also sent to Google.

## 9. Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the authority responsible for your place of residence or the authority responsible for us: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

## 10. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The current version applies.
