## 2. General

Kuiper Siege is a game for iPhone and iPad. We do not collect any personal data. There is no user account, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics or crash-reporting service, no tracking and no sharing of data with third parties. The app does not need an internet connection and does not open connections on its own.

## 3. Data stored locally

Game progress (completed levels and stars), crystals, research, daily bonus and settings (sound, display, language) are stored only on your device (the app's UserDefaults). We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app; you can also reset it under Settings › “Delete progress”.

**Transfers to third countries:** GitHub may transfer data to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; standard contractual clauses also apply. More information: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## 5. Crash data from Apple

If you have agreed in your iOS settings to share analytics data with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash and usage data (App Store Connect). We have no influence on this; Apple's privacy policy applies.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). Please contact apple-dev@stephankopp.de. Note that we do not hold any personal data from the app ourselves.

## 7. Right to lodge a complaint

You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for your place of residence or the one responsible for us: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

## 8. Changes

We will update this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The current version applies.
